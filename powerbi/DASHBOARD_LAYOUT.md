- Department
- Job Role
- Overtime
- Age Group
- Salary Range

### Main visuals
1. Attrition Rate by Department — horizontal bar chart
2. Attrition Rate by Job Role — horizontal bar chart; consider showing roles with at least 10 employees
3. Attrition Rate by Overtime — column chart
4. Attrition Rate by Salary Range — column/bar chart
5. Attrition Rate by Age Group — column chart
6. Attrition Rate by Tenure Group — column chart
7. Attrition Rate by Satisfaction Level — column chart

### Design
Use a restrained blue/neutral theme, consistent percentage formatting, clear titles, and no unnecessary decorative elements. Keep the dashboard readable at normal zoom.

### Validation values
The finished PBIX must reproduce the five KPI values above when no slicers are applied.
